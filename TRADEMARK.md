# Trademark & Naming Policy

**Effective: 2026-09-05 · Status: formal trademark search PENDING (see §3)**

## 1. The name

"MusicForge" and the project logo identify this open-source project
(https://github.com/simenty/MusicForge), distributed under the MIT license.

- The **code** is MIT — you may use, modify, and redistribute it freely.
- The **name "MusicForge"** is not part of that grant. You may refer to it factually
  ("compatible with MusicForge", "forked from MusicForge"), but you may not present
  your own build or product as "MusicForge" in a way that confuses origin.

## 2. Allowed / not allowed

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|:--|:--|
| Fencing/compatible-with statements | Distributing modified builds under the same name without visible change notes |
| Forks that rename (e.g. "XForge, a MusicForge fork") | Using the name for unrelated products |
| Verbatim unmodified redistribution with attribution | Implied endorsement by the MusicForge team |

## 3. Pending formal clearance

- A **formal trademark search** (CNIPA / EUIPO / USPTO as applicable) has **not yet been
  completed**. This document is an interim policy, not a registration claim.
- Until clearance completes: no merchandise, no paid services branded "MusicForge",
  and third-party distributions should state "unofficial build".
- External legal review is scheduled (internal deadline 2026-09-09). This section will be
  updated with the outcome.

## 4. Report misuse

Name misuse or confusing distributions: open an issue at
https://github.com/simenty/MusicForge/issues or see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact.
